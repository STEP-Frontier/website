Rocket Lab is preparing for two back-to-back launches within just three days, marking an unprecedented turnaround time for the company.

## Mission Details

The upcoming mission will deploy the final satellites in the Kineis constellation, aiming to enhance global maritime and asset tracking capabilities.

### Key Highlights:
- Two launches scheduled just three days apart
- Electron rocket supporting rapid reusability improvements
- Final Kineis satellites to enhance global tracking services
