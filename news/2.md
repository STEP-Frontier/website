STEP has successfully conducted a full-duration static fire test of its latest rocket engine, marking a major milestone in the development of the club's aerospace projects.

## 🔥 Engine Test Details

The engine test took place at the Tsukuba University Aerospace Testing Facility and lasted for a full **30 seconds**. Engineers closely monitored **thrust performance**, **fuel efficiency**, and **structural integrity**.

### 🚀 Key Achievements:
- Achieved **stable combustion** for 30 seconds
- Fuel efficiency improved by **15%**
- Successfully tested new **composite engine nozzle**
- Structural integrity remained within safety margins

## 🔭 Next Steps

The STEP team will now analyze the collected data and make necessary optimizations before the next round of testing. Future plans include a **suborbital flight test** scheduled for late 2024.
